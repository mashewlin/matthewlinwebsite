Research

My research spans molecular machine learning, molecular simulation, and experimental materials science.

Ionic liquid property prediction

May 2026 – present

Research Assistant · Nordness Group, Columbia University

I’m developing models using AttentiveFP and LightGBM to predict the physical properties of ionic liquids.

CO2 adsorption in metal–organic frameworks

September 2023 – April 2024

Research Assistant · Chen Group, University of Texas Rio Grande Valley

I performed Grand Canonical Monte Carlo simulations in RASPA to model CO2 adsorption in frameworks with open metal sites, and analyzed adsorption isotherms and host–guest interactions. Related publication.

Sodium-ion battery cathodes

June – July 2024

Research Assistant · Bocarsly Group, University of Houston

I experimented with component concentrations and cooling rates, then used X-ray diffraction and battery cycling to study the synthesized cathode materials.

Pore formation in nanoparticles

June – August 2023

Research Assistant · Uddin Group, University of Texas Rio Grande Valley

I studied the effects of hexamethylenetetramine and formaldehyde on nanoparticle pore formation, using X-ray diffraction and scanning electron microscopy to characterize the materials.
